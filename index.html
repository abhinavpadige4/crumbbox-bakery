Meet Our Bakers

The passionate artisans behind every loaf and pastry

Sarah Martinez

Master Baker & Founder

With 15 years of experience in artisan bread baking, Sarah trained in France and Italy before bringing her passion to CrumbBox. She specializes in sourdough and traditional European breads.

Michael Chen

Executive Pastry Chef

Michael brings expertise in French patisserie and modern dessert techniques. His creations blend classic techniques with innovative flavors and beautiful presentation.

Jamie Wilson

Bread Specialist

Jamie focuses on whole grain and specialty breads, experimenting with ancient grains and unique flour blends to create nutritious and flavorful loaves.
